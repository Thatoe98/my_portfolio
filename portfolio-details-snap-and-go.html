Project Overview

Snap & Go is a BYOD (bring-your-own-device), staff-less grocery checkout prototype: a shopper scans their own cart with a phone camera, the backend detects every item with a custom-trained YOLOv8 model, and checkout completes with a self-serve PromptPay slip verification — no cashier or barcode gun required.

I built the object-detection dataset from scratch: recorded product videos, extracted roughly 100 frames per class, and labelled bounding boxes in Roboflow. To make the model robust to real store lighting, I applied brightness (±25%), blur, and rotation (±15°) augmentation before training YOLOv8n for 50 epochs on a Colab T4 GPU.

Core Modules

Snapshot Inference

FastAPI endpoint decodes a JPEG snapshot with OpenCV and runs YOLOv8n with a configurable confidence threshold (0.80).

QR Payment Verification

Decodes PromptPay payment-slip QR codes (pyzbar) and cross-checks the paid amount against the detected cart total.

Product & Price Lookup

Detected class labels resolve to live product and price data stored in Supabase (Postgres).

PWA-Style Frontend

Next.js + Tailwind frontend built for mobile-first use directly from a shopper's own phone.

Technical Strengths

Dataset From Scratch

Training & Inference

  • YOLOv8n trained for 50 epochs on a Colab T4 GPU
